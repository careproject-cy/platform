Bringing a dog home, especially one from a shelter, requires some preparation to ensure a smooth transition. Here’s a list of must-have items for dog owners in Cyprus:

1. **Collar/Harness**: Choose a comfortable, well-fitting collar or harness. Ensure two fingers can fit between the collar and your dog's neck. Harnesses should fit snugly around the chest without restricting breathing. Collars are particularly useful for training larger, stronger dogs.
2. **Leashes**: Different leashes serve different purposes. A short leash offers better control during walks, ensuring the dog stays close for safety. Long leashes (5, 10, 15 meters) are ideal for training commands like "come." Avoid retractable leashes as they can encourage pulling. Instead, opt for standard leashes over 5 meters.
3. **Comfortable bed**: Provide a cozy bed for your dog. In hot climates like Cyprus, a cooling mat is essential. A designated resting place offers comfort and a sense of security.
4. **Food and water bowls**: Use stable, heavy bowls to prevent spillage and noise. Ceramic bowls are a great option as they are less likely to scare anxious dogs.

By having these essentials ready, you'll create a welcoming environment for your new furry friend, ensuring a comfortable and stress-free adaptation period.
