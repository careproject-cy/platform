🐾 **Lily** — rescued from the municipal shelter in Limassol.  
A shy girl with a big heart and a deep desire to trust.  
She won the hearts of her new family and is now living a happy life in the **Netherlands**, in a loving home shared with a cat! 🐱💕

![Lily](img/blog/2025-02-10-your-new-best-friend-awaits/dog-in-the-forest.png)
---

🐾 **Sandy** — a tiny bundle of joy from the municipal shelter.  
She flew to her forever family in **Germany**, bringing sunshine and happiness with her! ☀️

---

🐾 **Lucy** — found in an abandoned house and later cared for in one of Limassol’s shelters under our wing.  
This sweet girl finally found her safe haven in the **United Kingdom**. 🏡

---

How we wish we could share news like this every month.  
**Thank you for being with us** — because it’s only with your support that we can keep helping dogs like Lily, Sandy, and Lucy find their loving homes.  

And while they wait, we can at least make their life at the shelter a little bit better — with care, love, and the medical attention they so deeply need. ❤️
