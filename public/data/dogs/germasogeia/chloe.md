Chloe is a very shy girl who never rushes out for a walk on her own. Most of the time, she simply stands on a ledge in her kennel, quietly watching the people who pass by or the cleaner tidying her space.

She gets nervous when someone tries to take her out, but once she’s outside, everything changes. Curiosity takes over, and she starts enjoying her surroundings—walking happily, sniffing the grass, and exploring the world at her own pace. She gets along well with other dogs, and having a companion on walks gives her a noticeable boost of confidence.

If you sit with her, she will slowly come closer and stay by your side, waiting for gentle pets. Unlike many dogs, she isn’t too focused on treats—she simply finds comfort in being near someone she trusts.

Chloe needs a patient and understanding family who will help her feel safe and give her the time she needs to come out of her shell. Could you be the one to show her that the world isn’t so scary?
