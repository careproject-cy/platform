Baron was found in the Agios Athanasios area of Limassol in a heartbreaking condition and brought to the municipal pound. He had no microchip, no signs of recent care, and every sign of a life filled with suffering. 💔
We’re not asking if someone is looking for him—because it’s painfully clear no one is. He wasn’t just skin and bones from being lost; his body tells a different story. Scars and wounds cover him, including a deep one around his neck, likely from being tied with a tight collar for far too long. This wasn’t a beloved pet who went missing. This was a life of neglect—perhaps even abuse.
Despite everything he’s been through, Baron is incredibly calm and gentle. He doesn’t react to other dogs or cats, and all he wants is a quiet home where he can finally rest.

Baron is a senior boy who deserves to spend his golden years surrounded by kindness, not pain. If you have a peaceful home and space in your heart, please consider giving Baron the love he has never truly known.
