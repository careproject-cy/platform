Diamond is a stunning young black German Shepherd mix. While he’s very shy at first and doesn’t usually leave his kennel, he opens up beautifully in one-on-one situations. Once he feels safe, he’ll come out to play and show you his affectionate, sweet nature.

He can be overwhelmed in busy or noisy environments, so he needs a calm and patient adopter who will give him the time and space to gain confidence.

With love and consistency, this gorgeous boy will make a loyal and gentle companion.
