This beautiful boy was once too scared to leave his kennel. For nearly three years, he lived in isolation, without freedom or affection.

But things are finally starting to change. With the care and patience of volunteers, he now comes out of his cage and curiously explores his surroundings. Recently, he even went into the open area all by himself and happily sniffed around — a huge step for such a shy soul.

He’s still a little nervous around loud noises and other dogs, but he’s slowly learning to trust. His sweet personality is beginning to shine through, and he gently takes treats from your hand.

He’s ready for the next step — a home where he can continue to heal and feel safe.
