# About us

The CARE (Cyprus Animals Rescue Effort) Project is a nonprofit project that aims to help stray animals in Cyprus. We are a group of volunteers who are passionate about animal welfare and dedicated to making a difference in our community.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe haven for stray animals and help them find loving forever homes. We believe that every animal deserves a chance to live a happy and healthy life. By working together, we can make a positive impact on the lives of animals in need. We are helping local shelters and rescue organizations by providing food, medical care, and shelter for animals in need.
