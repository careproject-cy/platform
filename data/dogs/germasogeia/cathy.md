Cathy was found alone on the street, and sadly, no one ever came looking for her.

She is an incredibly affectionate girl who truly adores human attention. Cathy loves being petted and scratched and completely melts in hugs — being close to her person is what matters most to her.

She is small in size, making her a great fit for both a house and an apartment. Cathy is food-motivated and could benefit from losing a little weight.

Cathy gets along very well with other dogs, but her biggest joy is spending time with people. She would thrive in a home where she can be close to her human and receive plenty of affection.
