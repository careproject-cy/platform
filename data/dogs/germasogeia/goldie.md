Goldie is an absolutely adorable girl with the sweetest personality. She is incredibly affectionate and longs to be close to her people. All she wants is love, gentle cuddles, and a family to call her own.

Sadly, Goldie arrived at our shelter with a severely deformed front leg. We don't know what happened to her before she was rescued, but the injury cannot be repaired and causes her constant pain. For her wellbeing, she will be undergoing an amputation very soon.

While losing a leg may sound frightening, it will actually be the beginning of a new, pain-free life for Goldie. Dogs adapt remarkably well to life on three legs, and once she has recovered, she will finally be able to move comfortably and enjoy life without the pain she has been carrying for so long.

Despite everything she has been through, Goldie has never lost her loving spirit. She greets people with warmth, seeks affection, and simply wants to be by your side. Her resilience and gentle heart make her a truly special girl.

Goldie is looking for a family who will see beyond her disability and love her for the wonderful dog she is. In return, she will give endless devotion, gratitude, and unconditional love.
