Brando was found wandering the streets all alone. For several days, people kept seeing him by himself before he was finally brought to the shelter. We made every effort to find his previous owners, but sadly, no one came forward for him.

He is a young, purebred German Shepherd—abandoned like he was no longer wanted. Despite this, Brando remains an incredibly sweet and gentle boy. Shelter life is very hard on him; he cries often and doesn’t understand why he is locked in a kennel.

Brando walks well on the lead overall. At first, he may be a little excited, but once he realizes he’s outside and settles down, he walks calmly without pulling.

He is looking for a home where he can be the only pet, as he is not suitable to live with other dogs or cats.

Brando truly deserves a second chance—a loving home where he will never be abandoned again.
