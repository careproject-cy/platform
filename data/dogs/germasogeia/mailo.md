This charming little boy is around 3 years old and full of personality! Milo is friendly, affectionate, and incredibly sweet. He is fully healthy, neutered, and weighs around 9 kg.

Sadly, Milo’s story is one we see far too often with shelter dogs — he was found alone on the streets. Perhaps he had escaped before, and at some point, nobody came looking for him anymore.

Milo is a loving companion who enjoys people and brings joy wherever he goes. But there is one important thing to know about him: he can be an escape artist if left alone.

Once, while staying with one of our volunteers, Milo managed to squeeze out through a slightly open window while she was quickly collecting documents at the vet clinic. Thankfully, he did not go far! He was soon found near a café close to the clinic, happily sitting with a man and enthusiastically trying to invite a local cat to play — barking, wagging his tail, and clearly convinced they were about to become best friends 😉

So yes — Milo passed his cat test! However, if you already have cats who prefer a calm and quiet lifestyle, some supervision at first may be needed, as Milo can be quite enthusiastic in his attempts to make feline friends.

Milo would thrive in a home that understands his adventurous side and takes sensible precautions to keep him safe. In return, you’ll get a loving, funny, and adorable little companion who will fill your life with joy.

Could Milo be your perfect match?
