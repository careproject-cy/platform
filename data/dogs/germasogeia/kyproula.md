Kyproula was discarded after many years of breeding. Now that she is older, her previous owner simply gave her up to the shelter. We have no idea how many litters she has had, but we can only hope that she will finally spend her senior years on a soft, cozy couch — because that’s all she really needs.

She gets along well with other dogs and shows no reaction to cats. Kyproula would thrive in a calm, quiet home where she can finally enjoy peace and comfort.
