Bear is a truly special and charming boy with a story that could have ended very differently.

He used to wander alone, often seen without care or supervision. One day, he was poisoned and, in his final moments, came to a kind woman’s home looking for help. Thankfully, she acted quickly and rushed him to the clinic, saving his life.

Sadly, his previous owners denied responsibility, and Bear was left with no one. But despite everything he has been through, he has remained incredibly loving and trusting.

Now fully recovered, Bear is ready to find his forever home ❤️

He appears to be a mix of Chow Chow and Labrador, but he is on the smaller side rather than medium. Bear is friendly, gentle, and gets along wonderfully with both people and other dogs.
All he needs now is a family who will truly care for him and never let him down again.
