This beautiful boy is calm and gentle, but also enjoys a good run and play session with other dogs. Olaf thrives on human connection — when he sees people at the shelter, he eagerly runs to the front of his kennel, hoping to be noticed and loved.

He has striking, unique eyes — one brown, and the other a mix of blue and brown — that make him truly unforgettable.

Olaf is also quite the jumper! He has managed to leap from one kennel to another just to be closer to other dogs, showing how much he dislikes being alone.

He’s longing for companionship and dreams of finding a person to call his own. Olaf would do best in a home where he’s not left alone for long and has the chance to feel loved and included every day.
