Bella was found all alone, frightened and unsure of the world around her. When she first arrived at the shelter, everything felt overwhelming for her, she would tremble and was even afraid to step outside.

But with time, patience, and love, Bella slowly began to blossom. She learned that she was safe, and now she happily goes for walks and enjoys exploring her surroundings.

What Bella loves most of all is being close to people. She absolutely adores being picked up, cuddled, and gently stroked. Human affection makes her feel calm and secure. Bella is a truly people-oriented girl who thrives on love and attention.

She is ready to bring warmth, loyalty, and endless cuddles to her future family.
