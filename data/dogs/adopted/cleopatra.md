Cleopatra is an absolutely loving and affectionate girl who adores people and lives for cuddles. She’s super friendly with everyone she meets and is always excited to say hello! Cleopatra is very food-oriented, which makes her eager to learn and train — she’s currently working on basic commands like “sit” and learning not to jump up in excitement.

Due to her strength and enthusiasm, she’s not recommended for homes with very young children, as she might accidentally knock them over when greeting. Cleopatra walks well on a lead and enjoys her walks.

She was found pregnant in Agia Fyla and has been through a lot, but her heart is still full of love. Unfortunately, she’s not good with other dogs, so she would thrive as the only pet in the household.

If you’re looking for a loyal, cuddly, and happy companion, Cleopatra might be your perfect match!
