When Nora first arrived at the shelter, she was terrified of everything. She would hide under her kennel for weeks, too scared to come out or trust anyone. But thanks to the patience, love, and care of our volunteers, Nora slowly began to open up — and what a sweet, gentle soul she turned out to be.
Now she loves soft pats and kind words, and she’s great with other dogs. Nora is still a little cautious with new people, but with love and understanding, she blossoms more and more every day. She just needs a calm home and someone who will show her that people can be kind.
