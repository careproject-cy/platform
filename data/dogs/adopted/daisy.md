She is a wonderful, active girl with a big heart. Daisy absolutely loves cuddles, enjoys sitting in your arms, and happily plays with other dogs. She has a very affectionate and joyful personality.

The first days in a kennel were hard for her, like for many dogs. It was stressful and confusing at first, but Daisy is adapting and learning to feel safe again.

She deserves a loving home where she can finally feel secure and be the happy dog she truly is 💛
