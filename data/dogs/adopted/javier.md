Meet Javier! Rescued from the forest with a broken leg, he was brought to a shelter and later moved to foster care. After a successful surgery, his leg fully recovered, and now he is a healthy, happy dog.

Javier is a social and sensitive dog. He gets along well with other dogs and has a natural talent for assisting in puppy socialization. While initially shy around people, he becomes affectionate and trusting with time. He is good with children but may be a little wary. Cats intrigue him — he may chase them, but not with harmful intent. He knows how to walk on a long leash (7m+ recommended), responds to basic commands, and enjoys nose work, car rides, and cuddling on the couch. Javier would thrive in a home with another dog and a patient owner who understands his gentle nature.
