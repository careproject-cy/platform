Indy is the loving mum of six beautiful puppies. Sadly, she became another victim of irresponsible ownership — after not being spayed in time, she was abandoned when she became pregnant.

She arrived at the shelter with seven puppies, but heartbreakingly, one did not survive. Despite the overwhelming stress of shelter life, Indy has been an incredibly devoted mother, doing everything she could to care for her babies.

When Indy first arrived, she was severely underweight and exhausted, yet she still gave all her strength to her puppies. Now that her little ones have grown and moved to a separate kennel, it is finally Indy’s turn to be cared for — she has done an amazing job and deserves love, safety, and rest.

Indy is a gentle and obedient girl. It is clear that she may have experienced mistreatment in the past — she sometimes flinches at sudden movements — but despite everything, she still seeks human affection and wants to trust.

She currently weighs 23 kg, but we are still helping her regain a healthy weight. Once fully recovered, we expect her to be around 26–28 kg.

Indy is ready to leave shelter life behind and finally experience what it means to be truly loved.

Could you give Indy the happy ending she deserves? ❤️
