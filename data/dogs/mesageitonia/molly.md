Molly was found wandering the streets. We were happy to see that she had a microchip, but sadly, we were told that her original owner had moved away and left her with other people — and they didn’t want her.

So this beautiful girl ended up in the shelter. At first, she was very scared, but she has since relaxed and shown us her sweet, gentle personality. Molly gets along wonderfully with other dogs and with children — a truly lovely girl ready for a loving home.
