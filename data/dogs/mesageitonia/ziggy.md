Ziggy is a charming and handsome boy who was found wandering the streets. We even know that he was once sold to someone, but they never came to pick him up — and he didn’t have a microchip. We believe we can find him a much better family, one that would never let him end up alone on the streets again.

He can be a bit cautious with new people, but he quickly forms a strong bond with the volunteers he sees regularly. Just look at that smile — maybe he’ll steal your heart?
