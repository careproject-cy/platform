Ramos is a gentle senior boy with the kindest eyes and a heart full of love. At around 10 years old, all he dreams of is a peaceful life surrounded by people who will finally give him the care he has always deserved.

When Ramos was rescued, he was in heartbreaking condition. He was painfully thin, covered in wounds and abrasions, suffering from leishmaniasis, and his ears had been left untreated for far too long. It was clear that he had been neglected and had endured a very difficult life.

Despite everything he has been through, Ramos has remained calm, gentle, and trusting. He doesn't ask for much—just a soft bed, kind hands, and someone to love him.

Ramos has already survived the hardest part of his journey. Now, our greatest wish is for him to spend his golden years in a warm, loving home instead of a shelter. Every senior dog deserves to know what it feels like to belong to a family, and Ramos is no exception.

If you're looking for a loyal companion with a gentle soul, Ramos will reward your kindness with endless gratitude and unconditional love.
