Silver is a stunning young boy, who was found tied outside the gate of the pound in April 2025. Since 
then, he has been living at the shelter, slowly learning that people can be kind.

He is incredibly gentle and shy, showing no signs of aggression. While he currently prefers the safety of his kennel, Silver will come to the front for treats and gentle strokes, showing us that trust is building day by day.

He has been out in the open area before and ran freely, showing his playful side — even if he still keeps some distance from people.

Silver would thrive with the help of a patient trainer or experienced foster who can guide him at his own pace and help him feel safe enough to explore the world beyond his kennel. With time, kindness, and stability, this beautiful boy has so much potential to open up.
