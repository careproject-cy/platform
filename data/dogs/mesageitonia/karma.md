Karma was found with a broken spine, and at that moment her future looked very uncertain. She was immediately taken for surgery, and her long recovery began in the care of a devoted volunteer.

Later, another volunteer took her in and continued helping her every day with physiotherapy, patience, and a lot of love. It was not an easy journey, but Karma never gave up.

Step by step, she grew stronger. And in the end, she amazed everyone. Despite all expectations, Karma showed the vets and all of us that she can run, play, and enjoy life just like any other dog.

Karma is about a year old and has her whole life ahead of her. Now she is ready for the most important thing she has been waiting for a home of her own.

She didn’t fight this hard for nothing. Karma truly deserves a loving family who will cherish her forever 🤍
